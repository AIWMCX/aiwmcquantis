AIWMC Quantis: Voice & Content Standards

## Core Principles
1. **No Hype:** We never use "disrupt," "revolutionary," or "game-changer."
2. **Data-First:** Projections are estimates, not prophecies. Acknowledge uncertainty.
3. **Operator Language:** We speak to people who run businesses, not just those who dream of them.

## The "Anti-Theater" Post Formula
- **Hook:** A provocative claim about business failure.
- **Insight:** Why traditional planning (spreadsheets) fails.
- **Example:** A real-world stress test result.
- **CTA:** Drive to `/examples` or `/app`.

## SEO Writing
- Every article must solve a "Pain Query" (e.g., "Why do coffee shops fail?").
- Internal linking must follow the **Hub-and-Spoke** model: Blog → Examples → App.
