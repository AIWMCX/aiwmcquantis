# Quantis Decision Validation Sprint — Offer Pack + Aug 11–15 War Room

Saved verbatim from the founder's 2026-08-11 working session. Source of truth for offer scope, copy, and the Aug 15 revenue gate criteria — reference this before changing anything on the conversion page or in outreach.

## Offer

**Quantis Decision Validation Sprint** — founding price $149 one-time, first five paid buyers. Target 48-hour delivery, supported cases only, human numeric-integrity review. Test price after five useful paid deliveries: $249. Separate from the Free/Plus/Pro SaaS plans — a service-assisted offer, not a new subscription tier.

**Fulfillment model**: manual — Stripe Payment Link (or invoice) + founder-run Quantis analysis + manual numeric review + PDF/report + 30-minute explanation call. Does **not** depend on the existing subscription/webhook/entitlement pipeline (separate open question, tracked independently, not a blocker for this offer).

## What the buyer is buying

Not "access to AI." A bounded review of a real near-term decision: which assumptions it depends on, which are most fragile, downside/base/upside cases, break-even/payback/runway where applicable, what to verify before committing money.

## Deliverables

1. 20-minute structured intake
2. Business-model/archetype fit check
3. Assumption ledger with origin labels
4. Base/downside/upside analysis
5. Break-even, runway, payback, key unit economics (where the model supports them)
6. Top five decision risks
7. Human numeric-integrity review
8. PDF/shareable deliverable
9. 30-minute explanation/decision call
10. Seven-day factual/formula correction window

## Promise boundary (Rule 4 — no false proof)

Promise: completeness, internal consistency, transparent assumptions, correction of genuine scope/formula errors.
Never promise: profit, demand, funding, investment return, or predictive accuracy.

## Best first buyers

- **Founder decision trigger**: US-based pre-revenue/early-revenue founder deciding within 30 days on launch, pricing, lease/location, hiring, or continued funding.
- **Repeat buyer**: independent consultant, startup advisor, or fractional CFO needing a defensible first-pass financial stress test for client work.

## Outreach copy

**Cold email (under 90 words)**
> Subject: quick question about [specific decision / client work]
> Hi [Name] — I noticed [specific trigger about their practice/company]. I built Quantis for exactly the part of the work where someone has to challenge the assumptions, run downside/base/upside numbers, and explain what would make the decision fail. I'm doing a small number of founder-reviewed Decision Validation Sprints at $149 and can send you a sample report first. Worth a look?

**Consultant variant**
> Hi [Name] — saw that you work with [specific client type / service]. Quantis is designed as a financial pre-mortem layer for the first pass of a client case: assumptions, scenario stress test, break-even/payback where applicable, and a decision-ready report. I'm opening a few $149 founder-reviewed pilot cases before expanding self-serve. Want me to send the sample deliverable?

**Warm introduction ask**
> I'm looking for one founder or advisor making a real pricing, launch, lease, hiring, or cash decision this month. I'm not asking you to "share the app" — if one person comes to mind, would you introduce us? I'm running a small $149 founder-reviewed Quantis decision sprint and can show the deliverable first.

**Diagnostic call opening**
> "Before I show you anything, what decision has to be made, by when, and what would make that decision expensive to get wrong?"

**Diagnostic questions**: What are you deciding? Deadline? What happens if wrong? Which three numbers are most uncertain? What do you use now? What has already been tried? What would a useful review change? Who approves spending?

**Close**
> "Based on what you described, the scope is [X]. I'll run the Quantis analysis, manually verify the critical numbers, give you the assumption/risk/scenario report, and walk through it with you. The founding price is $149 and the target turnaround is 48 hours. If that solves the decision you described, I can send the payment link/invoice now."

**Objections**
- *"I can use ChatGPT."* → "Absolutely for brainstorming. The reason to use Quantis is when you want the financial assumptions and scenario consequences structured and reproducible instead of asking a model to improvise the numbers. For the sprint I also manually review the critical figures before delivery."
- *"I already have Excel."* → "Keep it. Quantis is not asking you to abandon your model. The useful part is quickly challenging the assumptions, comparing scenarios, and producing a structured review you can inspect or take back into your spreadsheet."
- *"Why pay before Predicta is finished?"* → "Predicta is an evidence-system improvement we are implementing. I'm not selling it as live functionality today. The paid sprint is for the current Quantis financial stress test plus founder review. If external evidence is used, I'll label it explicitly rather than pretend the upcoming system is already in production."
- *"$49/month for something I use twice a year."* → "That may be the wrong product shape for you today. The one-time sprint is designed for a specific decision. The subscription makes more sense for someone running cases repeatedly, especially consultants. I'd rather match the offer to your actual usage than force a subscription."

## Aug 11–15 War Room

**Two tracks**: Track A (revenue every day — founder-led, not something I can execute: follow-ups, calls, proposals, payment asks, warm intros). Track B (trust/delivery safety — my lane: P0 security, billing, report integrity, production truth, paid-delivery QA, narrowly-scoped Predicta work already on the critical path). If a technical task doesn't unblock a sale, prevent customer harm, or protect the paid deliverable, it is not an Aug 15 priority.

**Contact volume**: net-new cold email capped at 20/week (deliverability constraint on a young domain). Target 10 total sales actions/day across warm intros, follow-ups, LinkedIn/DM, direct email, existing leads, partner messages, active conversations. Follow-up always before new cold email.

**Daily allocation**: 60% selling, 25% paid-delivery prep/real delivery, 15% product fixes (payment/report/trust/security/conversion blockers only).

**Aug 11 — Lock the offer and list**: freeze scope; verify one working payment mechanism in test mode (production only after its safety check); prepare one real honest sample report for a supported business model; create one conversion page/shareable offer doc; build top-40 prospect queue (15 consultants/CFOs/advisors, 15 founders with near-term decisions, 10 partners/warm-intro sources); send overdue follow-ups; send first outreach; publish one educational proof post with a direct CTA.

**Aug 12 — Conversations, not impressions**: follow up all engaged prospects; 10 sales actions; ask 5 warm contacts for a specific introduction; 2–4 diagnostic calls if available; on every qualified call identify decision+deadline+failure mode+current workaround, state the $149 offer, ask for payment when fit is clear; log every objection verbatim; fix only the single conversion blocker repeated by multiple buyers.

**Aug 13 — Proposal and proof day**: follow up every warm conversation; send payment/proposal immediately to qualified prospects; 10 sales actions; user-test with one founder + one consultant if possible; turn the strongest objection into a product fix (if real), a clarification (if misunderstanding), or a disqualification rule (if poor fit); publish a proof-led post (assumptions/scenario delta/evidence/risk, never generic AI language).

**Aug 14 — Close day**: no unnecessary feature building; follow up every open proposal; ask directly "is there anything besides X preventing you from doing this now?"; resolve only legitimate blockers; quote or decline scope creep, never silently expand; prepare intake/delivery calendar for any yes.

**Aug 15 — Revenue gate**:
- **PASS**: at least one external qualified buyer paid real money, transaction tied to prospect/source.
- **PARTIAL**: no payment yet, but multiple qualified proposals with explicit payment timing and a clear, buyer-evidence-backed repeated blocker.
- **FAIL**: no payment, no meaningful qualified pipeline. Diagnose the funnel stage that broke (no replies → segment/trigger/first-line; replies but no calls → problem/offer relevance; calls but no proposals → qualification/trust/problem-cost; proposals but no payment → offer/price/risk/urgency/payment-friction) and fix that stage, not the whole company.
