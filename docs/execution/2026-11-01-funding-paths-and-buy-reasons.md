## 2. The LLC question (not legal or tax advice; confirm with a lawyer/CPA)

- **Applying as an LLC is fine.** Neither YC nor Techstars blocks an application over entity type ([source](https://wisp.blog/blog/do-you-need-a-delaware-c-corp-to-apply-to-yc-or-hustle)).
- **Taking accelerator or VC money is different.** That expects a **Delaware C-corp**. The YC SAFE and most investor documents assume one ([source](https://seedlegals.com/us/raise/llc-to-delaware-c-corp-conversion.md)).
- **Choose by goal:**
  - *Goal = sell $349 reviews, open a bank account, sign contracts this month:* finish the LLC. It is enough for that, and you can convert later. A conversion has legal fees and tax steps.
  - *Goal = take YC/Techstars money in Q1 2027:* consider forming a **Delaware C-corp now** instead of finishing the LLC (Stripe Atlas or Clerky are the common routes). That avoids paying for formation twice.
  - *Either way:* if you issue founder stock in a C-corp, file the **83(b) election within 30 days**.
- **What to write on applications:** "LLC formation in progress (state X); will incorporate as a Delaware C-corp before any investment closes." That is true and standard.
- **Founder decision needed:** which state the LLC is in, and whether it has been filed yet.
