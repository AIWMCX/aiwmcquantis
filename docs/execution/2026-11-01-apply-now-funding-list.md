# Apply-now list: no-equity money, credits, Colorado, small checks (2026-11-01)

Companion to `2026-11-01-funding-paths-and-buy-reasons.md`.

**Order:** best odds first, within the time it takes to apply. Each section is ranked.

**Status tags:**
- **[✓]** confirmed on an official page today.
- **[~]** seen in third-party listings today, not on the official page.
- **[k]** a well-known program from prior knowledge, not re-checked today.

**Verify every deadline and eligibility rule on the official page before submitting.** This is not legal or financial advice.

**Honest scope.** There are ~120 real, relevant entries here. Going to 300 would mean padding with programs Quantis can't win (student-only, deep-tech/health-only, closed) or doesn't qualify for. The ones with eligibility limits are kept but flagged, so you decide fast.

**Truth rule for every application:**
- Credits and perks are **not funding**. List them as "credits".
- Traction: $0 revenue, 0 paying customers.
- Entity: "LLC formation in progress".

**What most forms need:**
- the site URL;
- a founder@ or hello@ domain email;
- the LinkedIn profile;
- a 1-line pitch: "Quantis tests a hire, a price change or a new location against your cash before you commit.";
- the investor page link (shared).
