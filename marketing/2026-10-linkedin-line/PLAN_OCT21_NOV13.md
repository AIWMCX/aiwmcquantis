## Who we are talking to (ICP)

**Primary:** the owner-operator of a service business with roughly 5–50 people (agency, clinic, trades, consultancy, studio), sitting in front of one irreversible decision:
- a hire
- equipment
- a lease or second location
- a big contract
- a discount

What sits underneath the decision:
- **Fear:** "I'll commit payroll and the work won't stay."
- **Pride:** "My team agreed, so it must be right."
- **Pressure:** the banker, partner or spouse will ask "what if it goes wrong?"

**Secondary:** fractional CFOs and advisors who have to defend an owner's numbers. They are the consultant offer.

**What earns attention honestly:** one counter-intuitive but true number, labelled illustrative, that the reader can check on a napkin. We never use invented customers, results, scarcity or ratings.

## How every post is built

1. **The first 2 lines (≤ 200 characters) carry everything.** That is all LinkedIn shows before "…see more". They hold a concrete tension and a number.
2. **The image is the thesis, in one sentence.** Navy, ivory and electric blue, with amber for risk, at 4:5. Arithmetic shown on the image is labelled *Illustrative example*.
3. **The body:** assumptions declared first, then the arithmetic, then the "so what".
4. **One question that is easy to answer in the comments**, either a binary choice or a number. It is never "thoughts?".
5. **Chain line:** each post ends by pointing at the next idea in the series. The series name is **BEFORE YOU COMMIT**, numbered #09–#19, continuing the #03 and #08 already published.
6. **Three hashtags maximum,** chosen from: #SmallBusiness #CashFlow #Hiring #Pricing #FinancialPlanning #FractionalCFO #BusinessStrategy #SmallBusinessLoans.
7. **Searchable words in the first line:** cash flow, hire, discount, break-even, payback, lender, second location. These are the words owners type into LinkedIn and Google.
8. **Engagement:** reply to every comment within the first hour. Never use engagement pods or bought reactions.

### Arithmetic check (every number on an image)
- **#09:** $6,500 ÷ 50% gross margin = **$13,000** revenue per month. The $6,500 is an assumed fully loaded cost: salary plus payroll taxes, benefits and tools.
- **#11:** contribution is $100 − $60 = $40. After a 10% discount it is $90 − $60 = $30. Then $40 ÷ $30 = 1.333, so **+33%** volume is needed for the same total contribution.
- **#12:** $30,000 ÷ $2,500 per month = **12 months** to payback.
- **#14:** 52 + 38 + 61 + 40 + 44 + 70 + 39 + 48 = 392 hours, and 392 ÷ 8 weeks = **49**.
- **#15:** $1,500 × 2 months before the first new job = **$3,000** out before anything comes in.
- **#17:** at −20%, $50,000 becomes **$40,000** revenue per month. With 45% variable costs, contribution falls from $27,500 to **$22,000**, a drop of **$5,500** per month.
