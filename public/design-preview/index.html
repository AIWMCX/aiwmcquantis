/05 — Good questions

Clarity starts
with the limits.

Does Quantis predict what will happen?

No. Quantis explores financial outcomes under specified assumptions. Scenarios are conditional models, not promises or verified future outcomes.

Who is it designed to help?

Consultants, fractional CFOs, founders, and operators examining decisions such as hiring, pricing, capacity, or expansion. This preview emphasizes the consultant workflow.

Can I explore it before creating a case?

Yes. Open the consultant demo to explore an illustrative decision before entering your own business information.

What if some of my inputs are estimates?

Identify them as assumptions and compare plausible alternatives. Do not treat a precise-looking output as stronger evidence than the inputs used to produce it.

Where can I check the methodology and plans?

See the methodology for how the analysis is framed and the pricing page for current plans and terms.
